Privacy Policy

Draft — last updated August 26, 2026

Stridetail ("we", "us") provides software that helps pet-care businesses run walks, visits, reports, and billing. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. It is written in plain English on purpose.

Two kinds of people use Stridetail

  • Businesses and their team — owners and walkers who sign up for an account and use the app.
  • Clients of those businesses — pet owners whose information a business enters into Stridetail, and who may receive report and invoice links by email. Clients do not need an account.

For client records, the pet-care business is the one deciding what to enter; Stridetail stores and processes that information on the business's behalf.

What we collect

  • Account data — name, email address, and login credentials for business owners and walkers.
  • Client and pet records — names, contact details, addresses, pet profiles, care notes, and vaccine documents that a business enters about its clients. Home access codes (door, lockbox, alarm) are stored encrypted and revealed only to the assigned walker during an active visit; every reveal is recorded in an audit log.
  • GPS location during visits — while a walker records a visit, the app collects the walker's device location to build the walk route and distance. Location is collected only during an active visit, never continuously.
  • Photos — photos taken during visits and pet profile photos, stored so they can appear in reports.
  • Payment records — invoices, deposits, and records of payments (for example, "paid via Venmo"). Stridetail does not process card payments and never collects card numbers or bank credentials. Payments happen directly between the business and its client on whatever method they already use.
  • Email delivery data — when we send report or invoice emails on a business's behalf, we process the recipient address and delivery status.
  • Basic technical data — logs and device information needed to keep the service secure and working.

How we use information

  • To provide the service: schedules, visit tracking, reports, invoices, payouts.
  • To send transactional email (visit reports, invoices, account messages).
  • To secure the service, prevent abuse, and debug problems.
  • To improve the product, using aggregate, non-identifying usage patterns.

What we never do

  • We do not sell your data. Not account data, not client records, not location history. Ever.
  • We do not use client or pet records for advertising.
  • We do not show one business's data to another business.

Sharing

We share data only with the service providers needed to run Stridetail — hosting, database, and email delivery providers — under agreements that limit their use of it to providing those services, and when the law requires it.

Retention and deletion

We keep data for as long as the account that owns it is active. Businesses can delete client records, pets, and documents inside the app. To delete an account and its data entirely, email us and we will delete it within 30 days, except records we must keep for legal or accounting reasons. Clients who want their information removed can ask their pet-care business or contact us directly.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit; access codes are additionally encrypted at rest and gated behind visit-time reveal rules with an audit trail. Access to production data is limited to what operating the service requires.

Children

Stridetail is a business tool and is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, we will update the date above and notify account holders by email.

Governing law

This policy and any disputes arising from it are governed by the laws of the State of Texas, United States.
