Good to know

Vaccination requirements

For everyone's health and safety, current vaccines are required. Dogs: Rabies, DHPP, Leptospirosis, Bordetella. Cats: Rabies, FVRCP. Other pets: please inquire.

Booking deposit

A 20% deposit reserves your dates and is applied toward your total balance.

Cancellations

With 14+ days' notice the deposit is refundable. Under 14 days, it becomes a credit toward a booking within 60 days. Under 24 hours, the deposit is forfeited.

Why in-home care?

Staying in their own environment reduces stress, keeps routines consistent, and gives your pet one-on-one attention — without the risks of group settings.
